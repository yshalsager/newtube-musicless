Musicless feasibility prototype

The optional downloaded vocals.onnx and accompaniment.onnx are the Deezer
Spleeter 2-stem ONNX exports from k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx. prepare_models.py makes
the frame dimension symbolic and stores weights as fp16 with Cast-to-fp32 nodes;
arithmetic and input/output remain fp32. Model weights are not inside this APK.
